I implemented diagnostic view B, Return reasons & cohort mix, using the approved synthetic dataset stored in Supabase. It inherits the overview’s original sale period, category, product and size filters and preserves the KPI contract.

Reason bars show returned-unit counts and shares, including Not reported. Monthly 100% stacked columns show reason composition by original sale month, with returned-unit population sizes and an accessible counts/percentages table. These percentages describe the mix of returned units, not the probability of a return.

Hypothesis: A shift toward Fit-related returns may explain the later cohort’s change in overall return rate. I compared Trousers in October 2025–March 2026 with April–September 2026, using six complete sale months in each cohort and the same eligibility rules.

Observed: the early cohort has 375 returns / 1,329 sold = 28.22%; the late cohort has 313 / 1,106 = 28.30%, a change of only +0.08 percentage points. Fit share rises from 175/375 = 46.67% to 163/313 = 52.08% (+5.41 pp). However, Fit returns per sold unit rise by +1.57 pp while other and unreported returns per sold unit fall by 1.49 pp. The offset challenges a Fit-only explanation: a larger Fit share does not imply a similarly large rise in overall return risk.

I also checked a visible confounder: product sales mix. Wide Leg Trousers changes from 189/1,329 = 14.22% to 134/1,106 = 12.12% of sold units. The app exposes the product mix table and permits narrower product/size filters. Reported reasons are not verified fit measurements; buyer differences and intervention effects are unavailable. Next evidence would include underlying return records, measurements, buyer feedback and a controlled evaluation of a size-guide change. No causal conclusion is claimed.

Independent checks against preserved raw source tables passed for 19 filter selections. I also tested local reason/month selection without changing headline KPIs, keyboard activation, one-month comparison unavailability and an empty cohort. Individual record access remains for the later detail layer.
